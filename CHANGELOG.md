## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-08-05

### Added

- First public UDR1-compatible relay implementation.
- Bounded delayed-copy scheduler, bounded deduplication, metrics, graceful
  shutdown, malformed-frame checks, and compatibility fixtures.
- Pinned PingTunnel production image for amd64 and arm64.
- RouterOS entry, exit, verification, and owned-object uninstall templates.
- Ubuntu installer, hardened systemd services, and uninstaller.
- Anonymized benchmark evidence, security policy, release checks, SBOMs,
  checksums, and attestations.
