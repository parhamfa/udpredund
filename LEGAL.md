# Responsible and lawful use

`udpredund` is intended for resilience experiments and operation on networks
that the user owns or is explicitly authorized to administer.

ICMP tunnelling can bypass assumptions made by firewalls, monitoring systems,
network providers, or organizational policy. Do not deploy it to evade access
controls, conceal unauthorized traffic, interfere with third-party systems, or
violate law, contracts, provider terms, acceptable-use policies, export rules,
or sanctions.

The operator is responsible for authorization, data handling, monitoring,
capacity, incident response, and rollback. The project does not provide legal
advice and cannot determine whether a particular deployment is permitted.

The software is provided under the MIT license without warranty. A successful
test does not guarantee availability, confidentiality, performance, or fitness
for a particular network.
